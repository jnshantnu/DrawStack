Lucidchart → draw.io bulk import

Step 1 — export from Lucid

Open lucid.app (logged in), press ⌘⌥J to open the DevTools Console, paste this snippet, press Enter. It downloads lucid-export.json.

If the console shows a CORS error, open the URL it printed for the document list directly in a new tab and re-run the snippet from that tab instead.

Step 2 — convert to .drawio files

Pick the downloaded lucid-export.json, then convert. You get a zip of .drawio files with your Lucid folder structure. Unzip it and drag the folders into Nextcloud Files.
